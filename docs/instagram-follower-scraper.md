# Instagram Follower Scraper

An Instagram follower scraper helps collect structured public information related to Instagram accounts and their audiences.

CoreClaw provides ready-to-use Instagram data tools for creator discovery, audience research, lead generation, and social intelligence.

## Available Data

Depending on public availability and product capabilities, fields may include:

- Username
- Full name
- Profile URL
- Profile picture
- Biography
- Followers count
- Following count
- Posts count
- Verified status
- Business category
- External website
- Public business contact information

Where supported, follower-list workflows may return public profile records associated with a selected Instagram account.
