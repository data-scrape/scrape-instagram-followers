# Instagram Profile Data

Instagram profile data can help marketers and developers understand creators, businesses, competitors, and public audiences.

## Public Profile Fields

Available public fields may include:

- Username
- Display name
- Biography
- Followers count
- Following count
- Posts count
- Verified status
- Business category
- Profile URL
- External website
- Public contact information

## Use Cases

### Creator Research

Evaluate creators by profile size, niche, public biography, and business category.

### Business Discovery

Identify public business accounts and available websites or contact details.

### Competitor Monitoring

Track changes in follower counts, biographies, categories, and account positioning.

### CRM Enrichment

Add public Instagram profile details to existing creator or business records.
