# Instagram Business Account Scraper

An Instagram business account scraper helps collect publicly available information from business and creator profiles.

## Available Business Data

Depending on public availability, fields may include:

- Business name
- Username
- Business category
- Biography
- Followers count
- Website
- Public email
- Public phone number
- Profile URL
- Verified status

## Common Use Cases

- Lead generation
- Agency prospecting
- Influencer outreach
- Competitor research
- Business-directory building

## Responsible Outreach

Use public business information only for relevant and lawful outreach. Respect opt-out requests and avoid unsolicited bulk messaging.
